## Connect

1. Open **SyncNos → Settings → Notion**.
2. Click **Connect** and select the pages to authorize in Notion.
3. Choose a **Destination page**.
4. Run one manual sync and confirm the result.

Enable auto-sync only if you want it. SyncNos can access only the Notion content you authorize.
If you cannot find the page, share it with SyncNos in Notion and refresh.
Alternatively, select **Choose by link…** in the destination menu, paste the shared page's link, and save.

## Sync location

SyncNos organizes content under the selected page automatically. You do not need to create or
edit a database yourself. Changing the destination page sends future syncs to the new location.

## Disconnect and troubleshoot

**Disconnect** removes the current SyncNos connection. To revoke authorization completely,
also remove SyncNos from Notion's connection settings.

If SyncNos cannot reach its authorization service, check your connection. If your network cannot
access the service, enable a network proxy and reconnect.

Sync failures do not affect local content. Fix the connection and retry.
