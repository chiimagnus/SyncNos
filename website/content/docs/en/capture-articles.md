## Save an article

1. Open the article.
2. Choose **Capture article** in the popup.
3. Check the content in the [local library](/docs/en/library/).

SyncNos keeps readable text and available details such as the title, author, and publication date.
After saving, use [highlights & comments](/docs/en/comments/) or [reader tools](/docs/en/reader/).

## Images

Control image caching under **Settings → General → Save web article images locally**.
Retry missing images from **More → Cache images** in the detail view.

## Xiaohongshu comments

When capturing a Xiaohongshu note, SyncNos saves comments and replies already loaded on the page.
It does not expand the entire comment section automatically.
