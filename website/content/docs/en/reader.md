title: Reader, narration & outline
description: Adjust reading, listen to articles, and jump to the content you need.
---

Open a saved article or video and use the reader tools under **More** in the upper-right corner.

## Reading

Adjust font, size, line height, content width, letter spacing, alignment, and theme.

## Narration

Use your browser's voice or a configured AI narration service. Choose a voice or speed,
or click a sentence to start reading from there.

## Outline

Articles and videos show outlines when headings are available. AI chats have a separate
conversation outline for jumping between user messages.

AI chats do not currently support the article/video reading layout or narration.
